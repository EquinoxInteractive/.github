## About Us

We're a small team of 3 developers who make games. Equinox Interactive started in **April 2025** with our first project, **Box Siege**, originally made as a one-month school assignment. What began as a simple idea has grown into a studio with a clear ambition: build games that are polished, purposeful, fresh, and original.

- **Vision** - To be a creative hub for game developers to bring bold, imaginative ideas to life.
- **Mission** - To challenge the monotony of mainstream gaming by delivering original and meaningful experiences.

For full team biodata and each member's role/tasks, check out our **[website](https://equinoxinteractive.vercel.app)**.

### Box Siege
A round-based local multiplayer 2D fighting game, built with Unity and C#. 2-4 players, 16 playable characters, rebindable controls, and a Sudden Death tiebreaker.

[![Play Box Siege](https://img.shields.io/badge/Play-Box%20Siege-FF6B35?style=for-the-badge)](https://boxsiege.vercel.app)

*(This is our first and currently only game - more info and downloads are on the [game's website](https://boxsiege.vercel.app).)*
